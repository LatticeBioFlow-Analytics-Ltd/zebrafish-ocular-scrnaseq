## Source and terms of use

These data are **publicly available** and were **not generated by this lab**.
They come from:

> **Emergence of neuronal diversity during vertebrate brain development**
> (Raj et al.) — GEO
> [GSE158142](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158142) /
> BioProject PRJNA664124

We downloaded the raw sequencing runs from SRA/ENA and reanalysed them for our
own research question. We make no claim over the data, and we are not the right
source of authority on how they may be reused.

**For terms governing reuse of the sequencing data, refer to the original
publication and to the SRA/ENA record.** Anyone wanting to use these data should
obtain them from the archive directly rather than from this repository.

The licence in `LICENSE` covers **this repository's code and pipeline only** —
the analysis scripts, configuration and workflow. It confers no rights over the
underlying data.

## What these files are

Not the files distributed by the original study. They are **our own
reprocessing** of the public accessions, produced by
[cellranger-cellbender-workflow](https://github.com/LatticeBioFlow-Analytics-Ltd/cellranger-cellbender-workflow):
136 SRA runs downloaded, grouped into 22 sequencing libraries, counted with Cell
Ranger and aggregated per timepoint.

The grouping matters. The 136 runs are 22 libraries each sequenced 4–8 times.
Combining re-sequencing runs of one library inside a single `cellranger count`
is required, because duplicate molecules can only be collapsed within one run of
the algorithm. Aggregating per run instead inflates the cell count roughly 2.75×
— so these files carry substantially fewer, and more accurate, cells than an
earlier per-run aggregation of the same reads.

| Sample | Timepoint | File | Libraries | SRA runs | Cells | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| day5 | 5 dpf | `data/day5_filtered_feature_bc_matrix.h5` | 9 | 60 | 51,231 | 66 MB |
| day8 | 8 dpf | `data/day8_filtered_feature_bc_matrix.h5` | 13 | 76 | 51,348 | 59 MB |

Both are Cell Ranger `filtered_feature_bc_matrix.h5` outputs — barcodes already
called as cells by EmptyDrops, not raw droplets. Cell counts are the sum of the
per-library Cell Ranger calls.

## Processing

- **Organism:** *Danio rerio*
- **Tissue:** whole-head dissociation — the analysis resolves ocular and neural
  compartments together. See the original publication for the dissection and
  dissociation protocol; we did not perform it.
- **Chemistry:** 10x Chromium 3' v2 (8 bp I1 + 26 bp R1 + 58 bp R2)
- **Cell Ranger:** 9.0.1
- **Reference:** GRCz11, Ensembl release 116, filtered to `protein_coding`
  (25,432 genes, including all 13 `mt-` genes)
- **Aggregation:** `cellranger aggr --normalize=none`, per timepoint

Per-library quality across all 22 libraries:

| | 5 dpf | 8 dpf |
|---|---|---|
| Cells per library | 3,684–6,361 | 2,164–8,473 |
| Median genes per cell | 686–863 | 598–720 |
| Valid barcodes | 0.970–0.983 | 0.966–0.971 |
| Fraction reads in cells | 0.490–0.563 | 0.331–0.466 |

## Known caveats

- **Ambient RNA is not corrected in these files.** Fraction of reads in cells is
  ~0.51 at 5 dpf but ~0.39 at 8 dpf and the ranges do not overlap, so
  contamination differs *systematically between timepoints*. Uncorrected, genes
  abundant in the 8 dpf ambient pool appear mildly elevated in every 8 dpf cell,
  which is indistinguishable from a developmental change. CellBender-corrected
  per-library matrices are produced by the upstream workflow and are the correct
  input for any cross-timepoint comparison.
- **Sample and stage are confounded at the aggregate level.** With one aggregate
  per timepoint, batch correction would erase the only contrast, so it is off by
  default. Using the 22 per-library matrices instead separates library from
  timepoint and gives genuine replication (9 and 13 libraries).
- **Depth varies roughly 1.8× between libraries**, which is why aggregation uses
  `--normalize=none`: `mapped` would downsample every library to the shallowest
  and discard real data.
- **Dissociation bias is inherited from the original experiment** and is not
  something this reanalysis can correct.
